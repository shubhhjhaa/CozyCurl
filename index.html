Shipping Policy

Delivering happiness to your doorstep

At CozyCurl, we aim to deliver your orders in a smooth and timely manner across India. Please read the following shipping policy before placing an order.

Order Processing

All orders are processed within 1–2 working days after order confirmation. Orders placed on Sundays or public holidays will be processed on the next working day.

During sale periods or high order volumes, dispatch may take slightly longer than usual.

Delivery Timeline

Estimated delivery timelines:

  • Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Kolkata and other metro cities: 3–5 working days
  • Other cities and towns: 5–8 working days

Please note that delivery timelines may vary depending on courier availability, weather conditions, regional restrictions, or unexpected logistical delays.

Shipping Charges

  • Free shipping on orders above ₹500
  • A standard shipping fee may apply to orders below ₹500

Shipping charges, if applicable, will be shown during checkout before order confirmation.

Order Tracking

Once your order has been shipped, tracking details will be shared on your WhatsApp number or contact details provided during checkout.

Delivery Support

If your package is delayed or you face any delivery-related issue, feel free to contact us through WhatsApp with your order details.

Incorrect Address or Contact Details

Customers are requested to enter accurate shipping information while placing the order. CozyCurl will not be responsible for failed deliveries or delays caused due to incorrect address or phone number provided at checkout.

Service Availability

We currently deliver across most serviceable pincodes in India. In rare cases where delivery is unavailable in a specific area, our team will contact you regarding the order status.

Contact Us

For shipping or delivery-related support, you can reach out to us through our official WhatsApp support.

Thank you for shopping with CozyCurl 💖

Return & Replacement Policy

Commitment to quality and care

At CozyCurl, we put special care into maintaining the quality and hygiene of our products. Since scrunchies and hair accessories are personal-use items, we currently do not offer returns or exchanges once an order has been delivered.

No Return Policy

We do not accept returns for:

  • Change of mind
  • Color preference issues
  • Incorrect product selection by customer
  • Minor variation in product appearance due to lighting or photography

Please review product details carefully before placing your order.

Damaged or Incorrect Product

A replacement may be considered only in the following cases:

  • You received a damaged product
  • You received the wrong item

To request a replacement, customers must contact us within 24 hours of delivery with:

  • Order details
  • Clear unboxing video
  • Product images showing the issue

Requests without proper proof may not be eligible for replacement.

Replacement Approval

Replacement requests are reviewed on a case-by-case basis. CozyCurl reserves the right to approve or reject any request after verification.

If approved, the replacement process timeline will be shared with the customer directly.

Important Note

Minor differences in color, texture, or stitching may occur due to lighting, photography, or handmade detailing and should not be considered defects.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

We're here to help

1. How can I place an order?

Simply add your favorite products to the cart, fill in your delivery details, and place the order through WhatsApp checkout.

2. Do you offer free shipping?

Yes, free shipping is available on orders above ₹500.

3. How long does delivery take?

Orders are usually delivered within:

  • 3–5 working days for metro cities
  • 5–8 working days for other locations

4. Can I return or exchange my order?

We currently do not offer returns or exchanges. However, replacements may be considered in case of damaged or incorrect products under certain conditions.

5. What should I do if I receive a damaged product?

Please contact us within 24 hours of delivery with:

  • Order details
  • Clear unboxing video
  • Photos of the damaged item

Our team will review the request and assist you accordingly.

6. How can I track my order?

Tracking details will be shared on your WhatsApp number once the order is shipped.

7. Do product colors look exactly the same in real life?

We try our best to display accurate product colors, but slight variations may occur due to lighting and screen settings.

8. Can I cancel my order?

Orders can only be cancelled before they are shipped. Once dispatched, cancellation requests may not be accepted.

9. How can I contact CozyCurl?

You can contact us anytime through our official WhatsApp support for order-related queries or assistance.
